How it works

Plug it in. That is the install.

  1. 01

    We mail a streaming stick

    It arrives already set up for your spots.

  2. 02

    Plug it into any TV

    One HDMI port. Nothing to install on the TV, no codes, no cable to a computer.

  3. 03

    Turn the TV on

    Your screen is there. It stays there all day and your staff never touch it.

What is on the screen

The spots your customers are headed for.

Live cameras on the roads, trails, rivers and slopes, from more than 30,000 public cameras across all 50 states. Snowpack, river flows and the forecast for the places you choose. Your logo and name on every view, and a message line for hours, specials and events.

You pick the spots from the UpCanyon app on your own phone and change them whenever you like. If you want help, we build the first list with you.

It fits taprooms and breweries, gear shops and rental counters, climbing gyms, lodges, hotel lobbies and vacation-rental offices. Anywhere people decide what to do outside today.

A few camera networks are licensed to UpCanyon for people using the app, not for business screens, and stay off them: Pennsylvania's PennDOT traffic cameras and the WHOI Martha's Vineyard observatory tower. Everything else on the map is available.
