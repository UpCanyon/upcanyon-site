How it works

Plug it in. That’s the install.

Step 1
We send a streaming stick

It arrives set up for your spots.

Step 2
Plug it into any TV

One HDMI port. Nothing to install on the TV, no codes, no cables to a computer.

Step 3
Turn the TV on

Your screen is there. It stays there all day, and your staff never touch it.

What’s on the screen

The spots your customers are headed for.

Live cameras on the roads, trails, rivers and slopes, from more than 30,000 public cameras across all 50 states. Snowpack, river flows and the forecast for the places you choose. Your logo and name on every view, and a message line for hours, specials and events.

You pick the spots. Choose what’s on your screen from the UpCanyon app on your own phone, and change it whenever you like. Want help? We’ll build the first list with you.

Who it’s for: taprooms and breweries, gear shops and rental counters, climbing gyms, lodges, hotel lobbies and vacation-rental offices. Anywhere people decide what to do outside today.
