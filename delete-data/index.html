Delete Your Data

UpCanyon — published by UpCanyon LLC on the App Store and Google Play.

Last updated: October 2026

Accounts in UpCanyon are optional. Whether or not you signed in, you can delete everything the App holds, in the App or with one email.

If you signed in: delete your account

  1. Open UpCanyon and tap the gear to open Settings.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap "Delete account."
  3. Confirm. Your account and everything backed up to it are deleted immediately: your email and name, your pins, lists, saved places, unit preferences, and the alerts tied to the account. This cannot be undone.

Deleting the account does not erase what is stored on the phone itself. Use the next section for that.

Erase what is on your phone

  1. Open UpCanyon and tap the gear to open Settings.
  2. Under Purchases & data, tap "Reset app data." This permanently erases everything stored on your device: preferences, pinned spots, lists, saved places, and trial state.
  3. If you created alerts without signing in, delete them (or turn off alert channels) before resetting. That removes the matching server-side records.

Or request deletion by email

Email hello@upcanyon.app with the subject "Data deletion request." If you have an account, write from the email address you signed in with. If you never signed in, no proof of identity is needed: server data is keyed to an anonymous install ID, not to your name or email. We complete deletion requests within 30 days (usually much faster) and confirm by reply.
