Delete Your Data

UpCanyon — published by UpCanyon LLC on the App Store and Google Play.

Last updated: August 2026

UpCanyon has no user accounts, so there is no account to close. The App holds only two kinds of data, and you can delete both — in the App or with one email.

Delete everything in the App (fastest)

  1. Open UpCanyon and go to Account → Account Actions.
  2. Tap "Reset app data." This permanently erases everything stored on your device: preferences, pinned spots, lists, home spot, and trial state.
  3. If you created alerts, delete them (or turn off alert channels) before resetting — that removes the matching server-side records automatically.

Or request deletion by email

Email hello@upcanyon.app with the subject "Data deletion request." No proof of identity is needed — server data is keyed to an anonymous install ID, not to your name or email. We complete deletion requests within 30 days (usually much faster) and confirm by reply.
