Live cams, weather stations, snowpack, rivers, and storm alerts for the places you actually go — built in Fort Collins for people who get outside.

See it, don't guess it

Thousands of live cameras show you the road, the trailhead, the summit. Real weather stations, SNOTEL snowpack, and river gauges give you the numbers behind the picture.

It watches your spots

Pin the places you care about and UpCanyon taps you when it matters: severe weather, storms inbound, fresh snow, a wildfire nearby, smoke in the air.

Put your mountains on the TV

Cast View turns any TV into a live conditions dashboard — cams, weather, and snow for your pinned spots, rotating while you make coffee.

UpCanyon reads from the sources that report the ground truth — NOAA and the National Weather Service, USGS river gauges, USDA SNOTEL snowpack, EPA AirNow, state DOT cameras, NIFC wildfire data — and shows you where every number came from.
