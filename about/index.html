About UpCanyon

Where this is going.

Ryan Nelson, founder · UpCanyon LLC · Fort Collins, Colorado

November 2026

UpCanyon is a free weather app with a live camera on the home screen, built by one person in Fort Collins. This page is about what it is for beyond the app: the company we want it to become, who we want it to employ, and what we want it to give back to the places it looks at.

The app comes first

Open it and you see the sky from a real camera near you, with the nearest weather station under it, radar you can scrub, snowpack and river flows. More than 30,000 public cameras, each credited to the people who run it, in all 50 states. The whole app is free and has no ads. Pro is for people who keep track of more than three places, and nothing renews on its own. If the app is not good, nothing else on this page happens, so that is the job every day.

Who it should employ

Fort Collins runs on people who are very good at something and still do the math on rent every month. The line cook with a master’s degree. The field biologist between grants. The adjunct who teaches three sections and bikes to a second job. They are a big part of why this town feels the way it does, and the economy here does not pay them for it.

UpCanyon’s goal, past being a good app, is to become a company that hires those people, in Fort Collins, at a wage that lets them stay. Not a satellite office. Not contractors in another time zone. People who already live here and know which pass closes first.

How a weather app pays a salary

We are not going to pretend an app pays salaries by magic, so here is the arithmetic we run. After the app stores take their share, a year of Pro at $44.99 leaves about $38. A good job here, with health insurance, costs about $80,000 a year all in. So one full-time job is roughly 2,100 people paying for Pro, or about 55 businesses running UpCanyon on a TV in their shop, or some mix of the two.

The first job is the founder’s, because a company that cannot pay its founder cannot hire anyone. After that, every hire is in Fort Collins. When we hire, we will say so on this page.

Community, not just customers

The cameras people care about most are not the state’s. They are the one on a neighbor’s riverbank, the one a harbor office pointed at its own docks, the one a research station keeps running through the winter. Every one of those is in the app because a person said yes, and every one is credited to that person by name, under rules they set. A camera on a neighbor’s riverbank is worth more to this town than a hundred interstate cameras.

If you have a camera pointed at something people check before they go, a river, a pass, a trailhead, a harbor, we want it in the app with your name on it. You set how often it refreshes, and you can take it back with one email. How we treat cameras is written down. Backyard weather stations that share their data already show up on the map, credited.

The things people do outside here

We want to support the outdoor life of this place: the Colorado Marathon coming down the Poudre Canyon, the Horsetooth Half, fishing on the Poudre, climbing up the Poudre Canyon, at Red Feather and in the Park, hunting seasons on the Front Range, the gravel rides and the open-water swims. Right now that means what we can do for free: a live camera and the conditions for race morning, river flows for the weekend, a screen at the start line. When the company can write checks, it will, and those checks go to events here first.

What we will not do

  • No ad networks, no banners, nothing that tracks you. If a local business ever backs a camera or a race-day screen, its name is on it in plain words, and that is all it buys.
  • Nothing renews on its own. A trial ends and you are on Free. Your pins stay saved.
  • Your location fetches your weather and nothing else. It is not sold, shared, or kept.
  • No camera is ever recorded. The app shows the current frame and keeps nothing.
  • No growth that moves the jobs somewhere else. We would rather grow slowly on subscriptions than grow fast on money that wants the company to leave Fort Collins.

How to help

If you want this to be a company that hires here, the most direct thing you can do is pay for Pro and tell one friend who checks the pass before driving it. If you own a camera, host it. If you run a shop, a taproom or a lodge, put the conditions your customers ask about on your TV. And if you are one of the people this page is about, write to us. We would like to know you before we can hire you.
