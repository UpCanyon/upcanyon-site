Poudre Canyon weather

Poudre Canyon sits at about 6,450 feet in Larimer County, Colorado. This page has the National Weather Service forecast for that exact spot, and the 10 public camera sites, 3 snow sites and 3 river gauges nearby.

Colorado 14 follows the Cache la Poudre River from the canyon mouth to Cameron Pass. The weather changes a lot over that climb, so one forecast never covers it. This one is for the middle of the canyon.
