What UpCanyon Is

UpCanyon provides weather conditions, forecasts, webcams, snowpack, streamflow, reservoir, air-quality, and alert features for outdoor recreation across the United States, with the deepest coverage in Colorado's Front Range. The App aggregates data from government agencies, public networks, and licensed providers to help you decide when and where to get outside.

No Account Required

The App does not use accounts. Your preferences, pins, and lists live on your device. If you enable alerts, your alert rules are stored on our servers under an anonymous install identifier (see the Privacy Policy). Purchases are tied to your Apple or Google store account — not to anything of ours — which is also how they restore on a new device ("Restore Purchases" in Account).

Because there is no account, there is nothing to sign into and nothing to be locked out of. It also means that if you delete the App and its data without an active store subscription record, we have no way to recover your pins and settings.
